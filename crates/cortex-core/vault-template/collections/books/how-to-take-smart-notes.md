## Why this one

The book behind the "second brain" idea: notes that link to each other
rather than sit in folders. See [[Second Brain]].

## Notes

- Write in your own words; a note you cannot explain is not yours yet.
- One idea per note, so it can be linked from anywhere.

## Verdict

Slow in places, but the method holds up. Worth the four stars.
