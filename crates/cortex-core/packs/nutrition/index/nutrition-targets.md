What you are aiming at in a day. The row with `active` ticked is the one the
dashboard measures you against — keep a second row for days that are meant to
be different and move the tick rather than editing the numbers.

A target is only useful if it is yours. The numbers this pack ships with are a
starting point, not a prescription; change them, and if you are working to
someone else's plan, put their numbers in.
